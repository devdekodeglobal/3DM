================================================================================
                       3DM | INVESTOR & IPR PITCH
================================================================================

--- UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITION (USP) & DIFFERENTIATOR ---

3DM is a browser-based, zero-latency design suite built specifically for the 
Indian exhibition industry that merges high-fidelity PBR 3D rendering with 
precise, fabricator-ready 2D drafting. Unlike generic design software, 3DM 
features a live CSG-powered elevation editor and an automated blueprint 
generator that instantly creates fully dimensioned, technical layouts (Top 
and elevation views) in real-time. This eliminates redundant CAD drafting, 
slashes client pitch revisions from days to minutes, and runs flawlessly on 
low-spec laptops without expensive hardware.


--- COMPARISON WITH INDIAN PLATFORMS ---

* Foyr Neo (Chennai/Global)
  While Foyr Neo is India’s leading cloud-based interior design platform, it 
  is built for general residential/commercial spaces and relies on slow, 
  queue-based cloud rendering. It lacks 3DM's instant local browser sync, 
  custom wall-cutout engineering, and automated technical blueprints for 
  onsite fabrication.

* Infurnia (Bangalore)
  A cloud-based BIM platform designed for Indian modular kitchens and 
  home-building architects. While architecturally capable, Infurnia has a 
  steep learning curve, is tailored strictly for housing construction (such as 
  wardrobes and kitchen cabinets), and lacks the rapid, drag-and-drop workflow 
  essential for temporary exhibition stall setups.

* Flipspaces / VIZSTORE (Mumbai)
  A technology-driven commercial design platform focusing on virtual office 
  walkthroughs. Unlike 3DM, Flipspaces is primarily a sales tool for corporate 
  office pitches rather than a precise, production-level engineering designer 
  for fabrication yards.
